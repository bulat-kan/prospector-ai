# Prospector AI -- Product Vision (v2)

## Mission

Prospector AI is a **mobile-first Field Sales Operating System** built
around the principles of **Fanatical Prospecting**.

Its purpose is to help a Spectrum Business Account Executive know **who
to visit, what to say, and what to do next** every day.

Salesforce remains the **system of record**. Prospector AI is the
**system of execution**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Principles

-   Mobile first
-   Fanatical Prospecting first
-   Behaviors over reports
-   Protect Golden Time
-   Plan tomorrow today
-   30-second interaction rule
-   Enter data once
-   AI assists, never distracts
-   Keep pilot cost under \$10/month

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Product Goal

Answer one question every morning:

> What should I do today to maximize my chance of making sales?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Daily Workflow

Evening: - Prepare tomorrow's call list - Prepare tomorrow's
door-knocking route - Review follow-ups - Prepare appointments

Morning: - Open Today's Mission - Execute the current time block - Log
activities - Repeat

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prospecting Modes

## Territory Prospecting

Door knocking only inside assigned territory.

## Remote Prospecting

Cold calls, emails and texts anywhere Spectrum Business service is
available.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Today's Mission

The home screen should guide execution:

-   Current time block
-   Daily goals
-   Golden Time status
-   Next Best Action
-   Call list
-   Door-knocking route
-   Follow-ups
-   Appointment reminders
-   High-level commission progress

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Activities

Activities become the heart of the application.

Examples: - Walk-in - Cold Call - Email - Text - Appointment - Referral
Visit - Networking

Every activity may create follow-ups, opportunities and future coaching
insights.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Knowledge

Store: - Objections - Winning responses - Discovery questions -
Gatekeeper strategies - Opening lines - Industry playbooks - Referral
strategies - Fanatical Prospecting lessons

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Lead Research Workflow

Salesforce Export → AI Research Workspace (ChatGPT / Claude /
Perplexity) → Enriched CSV → Import into Prospector AI

Prospector AI focuses on execution, not web scraping.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mobile First

Current primary device: - Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra

Future: - Android - iPad - iPhone

Every workflow should be completable in about 30 seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Voice First

Future flow:

Record → Transcribe → AI Summary → Structured Data → User Review

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Cost Strategy

Pilot target: Less than \$10/month.

Use AI only where it adds value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Technology Roadmap

Today: - Streamlit - Python - SQLAlchemy - SQLite

Future: - FastAPI - PostgreSQL / Supabase - React Native (Expo)

Keep business logic reusable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Product Identity

Prospector AI is not a traditional CRM.

It is a Field Sales Operating System that helps Account Executives
consistently perform the daily behaviors required to achieve quota.
